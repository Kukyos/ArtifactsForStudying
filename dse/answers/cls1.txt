=== KM Q3
<h4>Main steps</h4>
<ol class="steps">
<li>Choose K, the number of clusters (elbow method).</li>
<li>Pick K initial centroids.</li>
<li>Compute the Euclidean distance of every point to every centroid.</li>
<li>Assign each point to its nearest centroid.</li>
<li>Recompute each centroid as the mean of its cluster.</li>
<li>Repeat steps 3 to 5 until the centroids stop moving; stop.</li>
</ol>
<img class="fig" src="img/kmeans_flow.png" alt="K-means flow diagram">
<h4>Strengths</h4>
<ul><li>Simple to understand and implement; fast and scales to large data (time O(n k t) for n points, k clusters, t iterations).</li><li>Every point goes to exactly one cluster, and the clusters are non-overlapping.</li><li>Always produces exactly K clusters.</li></ul>
<h4>Weaknesses</h4>
<ul><li>K must be fixed beforehand.</li><li>The result depends on the initial centroids and may stop at a local optimum.</li><li>Sensitive to outliers (they drag the mean) and to the scale of the features.</li><li>Finds only compact, roughly spherical (convex) clusters, and forces every point into a cluster.</li></ul>
<h4>Applications</h4>
<p>Customer or market segmentation and targeted marketing, grouping similar documents or news, image compression and segmentation, siting delivery or pizza centres, recommender systems, anomaly detection.</p>
