=== PR Q16
<p><b>(a) No-cloning theorem:</b> no unitary operation U can copy an <b>arbitrary unknown</b> quantum state. There is no U with U|ψ⟩|0⟩ = |ψ⟩|ψ⟩ for every |ψ⟩.</p>
<p><b>(b) Proof.</b> Suppose such a U exists. Then:</p>
```gbox
U|0⟩|0⟩ = |0⟩|0⟩        U|1⟩|0⟩ = |1⟩|1⟩
Linearity:  U|+⟩|0⟩ = (1/√2)(U|0⟩|0⟩ + U|1⟩|0⟩) = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2
Cloning:    U|+⟩|0⟩ = |+⟩|+⟩ = ½(|00⟩ + |01⟩ + |10⟩ + |11⟩)
```
<p>These are different states (one is entangled, the other is a product with |01⟩ and |10⟩ terms), which is a contradiction. So no such U exists. (Equivalently, unitaries preserve inner products: ⟨0|+⟩ = 1/√2, but ⟨0|+⟩² = 1/2.)</p>
<p><b>(c) Why it matters for BB84:</b> Eve cannot copy Alice's qubits, keep the copies and measure them later in the correct basis once it is announced. She has to measure (or disturb) the qubits in transit. Because the bases are non-orthogonal, that introduces errors (about 25 % on the sifted key for intercept-resend), and Alice and Bob detect them by comparing a sample. The security of QKD rests on this theorem.</p>

=== PR Q20

=== PR Q21
<p><b>(a)</b> H on q0: (|000⟩ + |100⟩)/√2. CNOT q0→q1: (|000⟩ + |110⟩)/√2. CNOT q0→q2: <b>(|000⟩ + |111⟩)/√2</b>.</p>
<p><b>(b)</b> The <b>GHZ (Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger) state</b>.</p>
<p><b>(c)</b> P(000) = <b>1/2</b>, P(111) = <b>1/2</b>, and all other outcomes 0. All three qubits always agree.</p>
<p><b>(d)</b> It cannot be split as (one qubit) ⊗ (two qubits) in any way, so all three are entangled together. Yet any <b>two</b> qubits alone (tracing out the third) are just a classical 50/50 mixture of |00⟩ and |11⟩, with no pairwise entanglement. The correlation exists only among all three, which is why it is called genuine three-qubit entanglement. Measuring one qubit leaves the other two in a product state.</p>
