=== PRT Q1
<p><b>Shared state:</b> |Ψ⁻⟩ = (|01⟩ − |10⟩)/√2. Alice holds the first qubit and Bob the second.</p>
<p><b>Decoding rule.</b> Bob's Bell measurement (CNOT first → second, then H on the first, then measure) turns the Bell states into Φ⁺ → 00, Ψ⁺ → 01, Φ⁻ → 10, <b>Ψ⁻ → 11</b>.</p>
<p><b>Encoding gate for 11:</b> the shared state is already Ψ⁻, the state Bob reads as 11. So Alice applies the <b>identity I</b> (does nothing) and sends her qubit.</p>
<p><b>Resulting state:</b> (|01⟩ − |10⟩)/√2, unchanged.</p>
<p><b>Bob decodes:</b></p>
```gbox
CNOT:  (|01⟩ − |11⟩)/√2 = |−⟩ ⊗ |1⟩
H:     |1⟩ ⊗ |1⟩ = |11⟩          → Bob reads 11
```
<p>Full encoding table when the pair is Ψ⁻ (from the checker):</p>
<table class="tbl"><tr><th>Message</th><th>Alice applies</th><th>State sent</th></tr>
<tr><td>00</td><td>ZX (X then Z)</td><td>−Φ⁺</td></tr><tr><td>01</td><td>Z</td><td>Ψ⁺</td></tr><tr><td>10</td><td>X</td><td>−Φ⁻</td></tr><tr><td>11</td><td><b>I</b></td><td>Ψ⁻</td></tr></table>

=== PRT Q3
<p>|ψ⟩ = (|0⟩ + 2|1⟩)/√5, so a = 1/√5 and b = 2/√5.</p>
<p><b>1. Initial three-qubit state</b> [1]:</p>
```gbox
|ψ⟩ ⊗ |Φ⁺⟩ = (1/√10)( |000⟩ + |011⟩ + 2|100⟩ + 2|111⟩ )
```
<p><b>2. Alice's operations</b> [2]. <b>CNOT</b> with her unknown qubit as control and her half of the pair as target: this entangles |ψ⟩ with the pair, giving (1/√10)(|000⟩ + |011⟩ + 2|110⟩ + 2|101⟩). Then <b>H</b> on her unknown qubit: this mixes |0⟩ and |1⟩, so that measuring in the computational basis acts as a Bell-basis measurement. The result:</p>
```gbox
½ [ |00⟩(a|0⟩+b|1⟩) + |01⟩(b|0⟩+a|1⟩) + |10⟩(a|0⟩−b|1⟩) + |11⟩(−b|0⟩+a|1⟩) ]
```
<p><b>3. Possible outcomes</b> [1]: 00, 01, 10 and 11, each with probability 1/4. The outcomes alone reveal nothing about a or b.</p>
<p><b>4. Corrections</b> [2]:</p>
<table class="tbl"><tr><th>Alice measures</th><th>Bob holds</th><th>Bob applies</th></tr>
<tr><td>00</td><td>(|0⟩ + 2|1⟩)/√5</td><td>I</td></tr>
<tr><td>01</td><td>(2|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√5</td><td>X</td></tr>
<tr><td>10</td><td>(|0⟩ − 2|1⟩)/√5</td><td>Z</td></tr>
<tr><td>11</td><td>(−2|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√5</td><td>XZ (X first, then Z)</td></tr></table>
<p><b>5. Final state</b> [1]: in every case Bob ends with <b>(|0⟩ + 2|1⟩)/√5 = |ψ⟩</b>. Alice's copy was destroyed by her measurement (no cloning), and two classical bits were sent.</p>
